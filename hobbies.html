My Favourite Games

Online Game: PUBG

PUBG is a battle royale game where up to 100 players land on an island, search for weapons and equipment, and fight until one player or team is left. It needs quick decisions, teamwork and good map awareness. I enjoy playing it online with my friends.

Physical Game: Futsal

Futsal is a fast, small-sided version of football played indoors on a hard court with five players per team. The smaller space and heavier ball demand quick passing and close ball control. I play it with friends to stay active and build teamwork.
